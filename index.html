Statutory Compliance FAQs

Authoritative guidance on your legal rights, exemptions, and timelines.

Section 12 empowers you to demand complete deletion of personal data when consent is withdrawn or purpose is fulfilled. All downstream processors are notified immediately.

All direct identifiers (name, email, shipping addresses) and pseudonymous telemetry (Matomo profiles, GA4 analytics, ad cookies) are permanently destroyed.

Under Section 12(3), data required for delivery and warranty protection is retained until fulfillment is complete, after which it is automatically erased.

Section 36 of the CGST Act mandates preserving tax invoices for 72 months in an isolated, encrypted accounting vault with zero access for marketing.

Identity verification and statutory erasure must be completed within 30 calendar days, accompanied by an official audit certificate.
